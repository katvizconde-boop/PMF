Frequently Asked Questions

Your one-page reference for the new Performance Management Form System. Print it, pin it, share it.

🔐 Getting Started

How do I log in?

Open sevengen.vercel.app in any browser. Use your @sevengen.com email + the temporary password from HR (password123). Or click "Sign in with Microsoft 365".

I forgot my password — what now?

Email HR or use Microsoft 365 SSO. HR can reset it for you in under 5 minutes.

Can I use my phone?

Yes. The app is fully mobile-responsive. Works in Chrome, Safari, or any modern browser.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It's a website — no apps to install, no IT ticket needed.

✍️ Doing My PMF

How long does it take?

About 15 minutes for your self-assessment. You can save and finish later — everything auto-saves as you type.

Can I edit my answers after submitting?

No — once submitted, it's locked. But you can save drafts unlimited times before submitting. Take your time.

What if I want to skip a question?

Required ratings can't be skipped, but text fields are mostly optional. Required items are marked with a red *.

Do I really need to draw a signature?

Yes — it's your formal sign-off, legally equivalent to ink on paper. Use mouse or finger on mobile. You can clear and redraw anytime.

🔒 Privacy & Security

Who can see my evaluation?

Only: you, your direct manager, and HR Admin. Other employees cannot see your scores or comments. Period.

Where is my data stored?

In a secure cloud database hosted in Singapore (Asia region). Encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

Is access logged?

Yes. Every view, edit, save, and download is logged with timestamp and user. You can request the audit trail on your data anytime.

📊 After Submitting

How long before I see feedback?

Manager reviews typically 1–2 weeks; HR final approval 1 week. You'll get email + bell notifications at every stage.

Will I see my manager's comments?

Yes — once HR finalizes, the full feedback (ratings + justifications) becomes visible on your dashboard.

Can I download / print my PMF?

Yes. After finalization, click 🖨 Print / Export PDF on the form. Get a branded, signature-ready PDF.

Can my manager change my self-rating?

Never. Your self-rating is yours. Your manager rates separately in their own column.

🎉 Bonus Features

What are Kudos?

Public recognition you can send to anyone in any of the 4 companies, anytime. Categories like Collaboration, Excellence, Innovation. Visible on the Kudos wall.

Where do I see my career path?

Open your Profile → see Career Path section. Shows your role's ladder, your current level, target level, and skills needed.

How do goals work?

Set quarterly goals at cycle start. Track progress (Active → Achieved → Missed). Manager rates them at cycle end.
